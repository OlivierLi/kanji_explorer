How to review. Each card renders the baseline (all variants off) next to the variant for the selected character. Click any image to open a swipe-compare with zoom. Use the Tune sliders under a card to adjust that variant's look (the variant image re-renders as you drag; slider values are saved with your judgment). Choose Approve, Reject or Tweak (with a comment saying what to change), then use Export decisions at the bottom. python3 scripts/apply_stroke_approvals.py decisions.json switches approved variants on by default and writes their slider values into static/app.js.
Ink opacity and darkness are unchanged — per your note the current transparency stays; the exact-Kubelka-Munk “darker sumi” option is not offered here.
